The mouth–body link

Your mouth is part of the rest of you

  • Gum disease and diabetes affect each other. High blood sugar raises the risk of gum disease, and gum inflammation can make blood sugar harder to control.
  • Gum disease is associated with heart disease and stroke. Researchers are still working out how much of the link is cause and how much is shared risk factors, such as smoking.
  • Gum infection during pregnancy has been associated with early birth, which is one reason dentists encourage care in pregnancy.
