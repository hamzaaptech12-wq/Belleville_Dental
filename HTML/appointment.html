Before you come

  • Brush and clean between your teeth as usual.
  • List any medicines you take and any health conditions.
  • Note any sore, sensitive or bleeding areas to point out.
  • Arrive ten minutes early for your first visit.

How often should I come?

Many people are advised every 6 to 12 months. Your dentist sets the gap based on your risk of decay and gum disease.
