Prevention first, in plain language

Belleville Dental exists to help people keep their natural teeth and understand how their mouth affects the rest of their health.

What we believe

Three principles

Prevention first

Most cavities and gum disease can be prevented with daily habits and early care. Treatment is the second line, not the first.

Plain language

Good information is useful only if people can understand it. We explain terms and avoid jargon wherever we can.

Evidence over hype

We say when research shows an association and not a cause, and we point to the organisations that publish the studies.

Who it is for

Patients, students and practitioners

Patients get guides and tools such as the brush timer. Students and practitioners get courses, teaching cases and calculators. Everyone can read the research briefs and browse products.

The content is educational. It does not diagnose or treat, and it cannot replace a visit to your dentist or doctor.
